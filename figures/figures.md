# Excluded Figures

The following newly generated figures are derived from the project data but are **not currently included** in the final report narratives. They were requested after the visual audit.

1. **`fig15_genai_employment_bubble.png`**
   - **Description**: A bubble chart plotting GenAI Exposure Score (ILO 2025) vs. Traditional Automation Risk (Frey & Osborne 2013) by sector. 
   - **Highlights**: Bubble sizes represent total employment in 2024 (in thousands), giving a clear view of the *workforce volume* impacted by the shift from traditional automation risk to GenAI exposure.

2. **`fig16_cluster_wage_violin.png`**
   - **Description**: A hybrid violin/box plot showing the wage distribution for occupations within each of the three main clusters.
   - **Highlights**: Visualizes not just the median wage per cluster, but the spread, density, and ceiling/floor, making the inequality between the clusters much more visible than simple averages.

3. **`fig17_resilience_corridor.png`**
   - **Description**: A scatter plot of Automation Risk vs. Adaptive Capacity Score, colored by the projected employment direction (Growing vs. Declining).
   - **Highlights**: Explicitly charts the "Resilience Corridor" (high risk, high capacity) discussed in the report, highlighting occupations that remain resilient despite high technical potential for automation.
